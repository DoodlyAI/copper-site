COPPER

A personal goal and planning app, for private use by one person.

What it does

COPPER keeps a personal list of goals and next actions. Notes captured by voice or text are sorted into the right goal. Each evening it helps plan the next day: it suggests a few priorities, fits them into free time on the calendar, and adds them as blocks on a calendar of its own.

Who it's for

COPPER is a personal project used by its owner only. It isn't offered to the public, has no other users, and there is no sign-up.

Google Calendar

COPPER connects to its owner's Google Calendar to see the day ahead and to add planning blocks to a separate "COPPER" calendar that it creates. It never changes or deletes other calendars or events. See the privacy policy for exactly what it reads and how to revoke access.
